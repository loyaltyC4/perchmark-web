29 Apr 2026
ShipImprove

Neighbourhood IQ — block-level intelligence ships globally.

The single biggest expansion since launch. Every property in our 87-city footprint now carries block-level walkability, amenity density, competition, and future-development signals. Available on Portfolio and Institutional.

  • Walkability score per property, computed from open street data + amenity density within 800m radius (groceries, cafés, transit, beaches, schools).
  • Future-development radar — surface council planning applications within 800m of every watched listing. Flagged in advance of approval.
  • Competition density heat map — a tile-grid view of STR concentration per neighbourhood, with the over-supply zones marked in Reef Rust.
  • Noise complaint history — three-year council noise complaint count per address (where data is public). Useful for due diligence on potential acquisitions.

Disruption Radar — what's about to change near your listings.

A new daily check that surfaces construction permits, council applications, and noise complaints within 800m of every watched listing. The intel your local guests will start mentioning in reviews three months from now.

  • Construction permit pulls from council open-data feeds across NSW, VIC, QLD (AU); UK; California, Florida, Tennessee, Arizona (US); BC, Ontario (CA); Auckland, Queenstown (NZ).
  • Noise complaint trend lines — when complaints in your area spike, you see it before guests do.
  • Café / restaurant openings within 200m — these tend to lift weekend ADR by 6–11% within 90 days.
  • Configured as a Slack digest by default. Switch to email or webhook in your settings.
v 2.5
14 Apr 2026
ShipAPI

Webhook firehose — push our intel into your stack.

Institutional customers can now route any agent output into Slack, Hostaway, Hostfully, Guesty, Lodgify, or any HTTP endpoint via signed webhooks. No more polling.

  • Five new webhook events: regulatory_alert, pricing_window_open, comp_set_drift, acquisition_match, portfolio_drift.
  • HMAC-signed payloads so you can verify Perchmark sent them.
  • Configurable retry policy (1–10 retries, exponential backoff).
  • Replay log for the last 30 days — re-fire any event from the dashboard if your endpoint was down.

Ship

Pricing window alerts — when to push, when to hold.

A new agent that watches your top 5 comps in each market and surfaces the moments when the pricing window opens on YOUR listings. Apply with one click.

  • "Your Wategos place is sitting $45/night below the comp set" — with the comp set, the calendar window, and a one-click apply button.
  • Auto-applied (Portfolio+) on the schedule and lift you set per listing.
  • Walk-back protection: if the comp set retreats within 48 hours, your pricing retreats too. No leaving money on the table; no chasing a rate that never holds.
  • Exposure cap configurable per listing — never raise more than X% above the comp set median, even when the algorithm wants to.

v 2.1
3 Mar 2026
ImproveFix

Sub-hour competitor pricing alerts. Comp set tighter.

We tightened the alert latency from 4 hours to under an hour for the top 5 comps in each watched market. Also fixed a long-standing bug where listings with mixed-amenity flags (e.g. "pool" vs "small plunge pool") were being clustered together inappropriately.

  • Improved: Competitor alert latency reduced from ~4h to <1h on Operator and Portfolio. Real-time on Institutional.
  • Improved: Comp set normalization now distinguishes hot tub vs pool vs plunge pool vs jacuzzi; ocean view vs ocean glimpse vs walk-to-beach.
  • Fixed: Edge case where a listing with no review history and no calendar data was being assigned a default occupancy of 65%. Now shows "insufficient data" honestly.
  • Fixed: Slack digest was sometimes posting twice if the underlying watched listing was edited within the digest compilation window.
