01The deal in 30 seconds.

You pay us a subscription. We give you access to Perchmark and the data, intelligence, and AI agents inside it. You can cancel any time. We can suspend service if you abuse the system or don't pay. Neither of us is responsible for things outside our control.

Perchmark is a decision-support tool. It is not a financial advisor, not a tax advisor, and not a substitute for your own due diligence. Acquisition and pricing decisions are yours.

02Your account.

You create an account, you keep your credentials safe, you're responsible for activity on it. Don't share login details. If you suspect a breach, email security@perchmark.com.au and we'll lock the account immediately while we investigate.

Plans with multiple seats: each human gets one seat. Don't share a single seat across multiple users — it breaks audit trails and may breach compliance for institutional users.

03Acceptable use.

Things you can do:

  • Use Perchmark to research, underwrite, and operate STR properties — yours or your clients'.
  • Export data and reports for your own business use.
  • White-label reports under our white-label terms (Portfolio + Institutional).
  • Connect Perchmark to your PMS, your spreadsheets, your team's Slack, your custom dashboard.

Things you can't do:

  • Resell or redistribute the underlying data feed without an Institutional contract.
  • Reverse-engineer the platform, our agents, or our APIs.
  • Use Perchmark to scrape Airbnb, VRBO, Stayz, or any other source directly. We pay for the data via licensed providers; reusing our outputs to backfill your own scraping operation is a breach.
  • Run automated tooling that bypasses rate limits.
  • Build a competing data-intelligence product using our outputs.
  • Use the platform for any unlawful purpose, harassment, or harm.

04Your data, your output.

Anything you create on Perchmark — watchlists, portfolio links, reports, custom comp sets, API integrations — is yours. We do not claim ownership. You can export it any time. If you cancel, we delete it within 30 days (see Privacy).

Anything Perchmark produces (intelligence layer, market scores, regulatory data, AI agent outputs) is licensed to you for your business use under your active subscription. The license ends with your subscription.

05Payment + cancellation.

  • Plans are billed monthly or annually in advance. Annual plans get a discount and lock in the price for the year.
  • Currency: USD or AUD at the rates published on the Pricing page. We don't surprise-convert.
  • Cancellation: any time, from your account settings. Service continues to the end of the current billing cycle. No pro-rated refund on monthly plans.
  • Annual plan refunds: pro-rated for the unused portion if you cancel within 30 days. After 30 days, the annual fee is non-refundable except where required by law.
  • Failed payments: we attempt the charge three times over 7 days. If all attempts fail, we suspend service. We don't auto-cancel — your data stays for 30 days while you sort it out.
  • Add-ons (extra markets, query top-ups, additional seats) are billed at the next renewal cycle, not pro-rated mid-cycle.

06Trial and free tier.

Free Scout is free forever — no card required. Use it as long as you like.

The 14-day Operator/Portfolio trial gives you full access to either tier (you choose). No credit card. Trial converts to free Scout if you don't subscribe — we don't auto-bill.

07Warranty + disclaimer.

We do our best to keep Perchmark accurate, current, and available. We commit to specific service levels in Institutional contracts (99.9% uptime SLA). For self-serve plans, the platform is provided "as is."

Things we explicitly disclaim:

  • The data we surface comes from public sources and licensed providers. We can't guarantee every metric is perfect every minute. We can guarantee the source citation is honest.
  • AI agent outputs are advisory, not directive. The Underwriter Agent's "verdict" is a synthesis of our model — it is not a fiduciary recommendation. Don't sign a mortgage on it without your own diligence.
  • Regulatory data is current to the best of our knowledge at the timestamp shown. Councils sometimes update their rules without telling anyone. Always confirm with the council before making decisions with legal consequences.
  • Future projections are projections. The future is not the past. We model with confidence intervals; we don't promise outcomes.

This cap doesn't apply to: (a) wilful misconduct or fraud; (b) breach of confidentiality; (c) infringement of intellectual property; (d) any obligation that cannot be limited under applicable law.
